# Aurora Bank Credit Cards

Aurora Bank offers three consumer credit cards. All cards are issued on the Visa network and include
contactless payments, virtual card numbers in the Aurora mobile app, and zero-liability fraud protection
(see the *Card Disputes and Fraud* policy).

## Aurora Classic
- Annual fee: $0
- Purchase APR: 24.99% variable
- Foreign transaction fee: 3% of each transaction made outside the United States
- Rewards: 1% cash back on all purchases
- Minimum credit limit: $500

## Aurora Gold
- Annual fee: $95 (waived in the first year)
- Purchase APR: 22.49% variable
- Foreign transaction fee: 1%
- Rewards: 3x points on dining and travel, 1x points on everything else
- Points redeem at 1 cent each for statement credit, or 1.25 cents each when booked through Aurora Travel

## Aurora Platinum
- Annual fee: $450
- Purchase APR: 20.99% variable
- Foreign transaction fee: none (0%)
- Rewards: 5x points on flights booked through Aurora Travel, 2x points on all other purchases
- Includes airport lounge access (up to 10 visits per year) and a $200 annual travel credit
- Requires a minimum annual income of $75,000 to apply

## Fees common to all cards
- Late payment fee: up to $40
- Returned payment fee: up to $29
- Cash advance fee: 5% of the advance, minimum $10
- Cash advance APR: 29.99% variable, starting on the day of the advance (no grace period)

## Grace period
If you pay your statement balance in full by the due date, you are not charged interest on new purchases.
The grace period is at least 25 days from the statement closing date.

## Upgrading or downgrading
Cardholders in good standing for at least 6 months may request a product change in the app under
**Cards → Manage → Change product**. A product change does not trigger a new hard credit inquiry.
