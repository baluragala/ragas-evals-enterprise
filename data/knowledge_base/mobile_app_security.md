# Mobile App and Online Banking Security

## Sign-in
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) is **required** for all online and mobile banking users. Supported
  methods are an authenticator app, SMS one-time passcode, or a hardware security key.
- Face ID / fingerprint sign-in can be enabled after the first successful 2FA sign-in on a device.
- After **5 failed sign-in attempts**, online access is locked for 30 minutes.

## Sessions and devices
- Inactive sessions sign out automatically after **5 minutes** in the mobile app and **10 minutes** on the web.
- You can register up to **3 trusted devices**. Remove old devices under **Settings → Security → Devices**.

## Alerts
Customers can enable real-time alerts for purchases over a chosen amount, international transactions,
balance below a threshold, and new device sign-ins. Security alerts for password changes and new payees
are always on and cannot be disabled.

## Reporting a suspicious message
Forward suspicious emails to phishing@aurorabank.example and delete them. Do not click links in
unexpected messages asking you to "verify" your account.
