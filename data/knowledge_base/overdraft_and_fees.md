# Overdraft Policy and Account Fees

## Overdraft fees
- Overdraft fee: **$29 per item**, charged when a transaction overdraws your Everyday Checking account.
- Aurora charges at most **3 overdraft fees per business day** ($87 maximum).
- No fee is charged if your account is overdrawn by **$50 or less** at the end of the business day.
- **Grace period:** if you bring your balance back to $0 or more by 11:59 PM Eastern Time the next
  business day, the overdraft fees for that day are refunded automatically.

## Overdraft protection
You can link an Aurora savings account for overdraft protection. When your checking balance would go
negative, Aurora transfers the needed amount from savings for a **$10 transfer fee** (instead of the $29
overdraft fee). Overdraft protection transfers count toward the savings withdrawal limit.

## Opting out
You can opt out of overdraft coverage for debit card and ATM transactions at any time in the app
(**Settings → Overdraft preferences**). If you opt out, those transactions are declined instead of
overdrawing your account.

## Other fees
- Everyday Checking monthly fee: $12, waived with $1,500 in monthly direct deposits or a $5,000 minimum
  daily balance.
- Out-of-network ATM fee: $3 per withdrawal. Aurora refunds up to $15 per month in out-of-network ATM fees
  for Platinum cardholders.
- Paper statement fee: $2 per month (e-statements are free).
- Stop payment request: $30.
