# Savings Accounts and Certificates of Deposit

## High-Yield Savings
The Annual Percentage Yield (APY) depends on your balance:

| Balance              | APY   |
|----------------------|-------|
| $0 – $9,999.99       | 3.50% |
| $10,000 – $99,999.99 | 4.00% |
| $100,000 and above   | 4.25% |

- No monthly maintenance fee.
- Up to **six free withdrawals or transfers per statement cycle**. Each additional one costs $5.
- Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

## Certificates of Deposit (CDs)
| Term      | APY   |
|-----------|-------|
| 6 months  | 4.60% |
| 12 months | 4.40% |
| 24 months | 4.00% |

- Minimum deposit: $1,000.
- Early withdrawal penalty: **90 days of interest** for terms of 12 months or less; **180 days of
  interest** for terms longer than 12 months.
- At maturity there is a **10-day grace period** to withdraw or change terms without penalty; otherwise
  the CD renews automatically for the same term at the current rate.

## Deposit insurance
Aurora Bank is a member of the FDIC. Deposits are insured up to **$250,000 per depositor, per ownership
category**.

Rates are variable and may change at any time. Rates listed are current as of the policy effective date.
