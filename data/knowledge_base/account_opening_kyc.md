# Opening an Account and Identity Verification (KYC)

## Who can open an account online
U.S. citizens and permanent residents aged **18 or older** with a Social Security Number (SSN) or
Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) can open a checking or savings account online.

## Documents required
- A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Proof of address dated within the last 90 days (utility bill, lease, or bank statement) if the address
  on your ID is not current
- Your SSN or ITIN

## Verification timeline
Most applications are verified instantly. If manual review is required, it is completed within
**2 business days**. You may be asked to upload additional documents in the app.

## Minors
Teens aged **13–17** can have an Aurora Teen Checking account, opened jointly with a parent or legal
guardian who is an existing Aurora customer. Teen accounts must be opened in a branch.

## Non-residents
Non-U.S. residents cannot open accounts online. They may apply in a branch with a passport and a
valid U.S. visa, subject to additional review.

## Minimum opening deposit
- Everyday Checking: $25
- High-Yield Savings: $0
- Certificates of Deposit: $1,000
