# Privacy and Customer Data Policy

## What we collect
Aurora collects identity information, account and transaction data, device information from the mobile
app, and records of your communications with us.

## How long we keep it
Account and transaction records are retained for **7 years** after an account is closed, as required by
banking regulations. Call recordings are kept for **2 years**.

## Your rights
- **Access:** you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you in the app under
  **Settings → Privacy → Request my data**. We respond within **30 days**.
- **Correction:** you can correct inaccurate personal information at any time.
- **Marketing opt-out:** you can opt out of marketing emails and partner offers in **Settings → Privacy**.
  Opting out does not stop service messages such as fraud alerts and statements.

## Sharing
Aurora does **not sell** customer personal data. We share data only with service providers acting on
our behalf, with credit bureaus as required, and with regulators or law enforcement when legally required.

## Assistant data handling rules
Digital assistants (including the Aurora virtual assistant) must:
- never display a full card number, account number, or SSN — show at most the **last 4 digits**;
- never ask a customer for a PIN, password, or one-time passcode;
- never provide personalized investment, tax, or legal advice. Questions of that kind should be referred
  to a licensed Aurora Wealth advisor.
