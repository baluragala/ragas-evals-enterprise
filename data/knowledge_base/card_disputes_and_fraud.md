# Card Disputes and Fraud Policy

## Lost or stolen cards
1. Freeze the card immediately in the Aurora mobile app (**Cards → Freeze card**). Freezing is instant
   and reversible; it blocks new purchases but still allows recurring payments you have approved.
2. Call the 24/7 Fraud Line at 1-800-555-0199 to report the card lost or stolen and order a replacement.
3. A replacement card arrives within 5–7 business days. Expedited delivery (2 business days) is free
   for Gold and Platinum cardholders and costs $15 for Classic cardholders.

## Zero-liability protection
You are not responsible for unauthorized transactions if you report them within **60 days** of the
statement date on which they first appear. Zero liability does not apply to transactions made by
someone you gave your card or PIN to.

## Disputing a transaction
- You can dispute a transaction for up to **60 days** after the statement date on which it appears.
- Open a dispute in the app (**Transactions → select transaction → Dispute**) or by calling the Fraud Line.
- Aurora issues a **provisional credit within 10 business days** of receiving a complete dispute.
- Investigations are completed within **45 days** (90 days for international or new-account transactions).
- If the investigation finds the charge was valid, the provisional credit is reversed and you are
  notified in writing at least 5 business days before the reversal.

## What Aurora will never ask you
Aurora Bank will never ask for your full card number, PIN, one-time passcode, or online banking password
by phone, SMS, or email. If someone asks for these, hang up and call the Fraud Line.
