# Wire Transfers

## Domestic wires (within the United States)
- Daily limit in the app: $50,000. Higher amounts require a branch visit or a call to Business Banking.
- Fee: $25 per outgoing domestic wire; incoming domestic wires are free.
- Cut-off time: wires submitted before **4:00 PM Eastern Time** on a business day are sent the same day.
  Wires submitted after the cut-off are sent the next business day.

## International wires
- Daily limit in the app: $25,000.
- Fee: $45 per outgoing international wire, plus any intermediary bank fees. Incoming international
  wires cost $15.
- Cut-off time: international wires submitted before **2:00 PM Eastern Time** on a business day are
  sent the same day. Wires submitted after the cut-off are sent the next business day.
- International wires usually arrive in 1–5 business days depending on the destination country.
- You will need the recipient's name, address, account number or IBAN, and the bank's SWIFT/BIC code.

## Platinum benefit
Aurora Platinum cardholders who also hold an Aurora checking account get **two free outgoing wires
(domestic or international) per calendar month**.

## Cancelling or recalling a wire
- A wire can be cancelled for free before it is sent.
- Once sent, Aurora can request a recall, but recovery is **not guaranteed** and depends on the receiving
  bank. A recall request costs $30.

## Fraud warning
Never send a wire to someone you have not met in person who pressures you to act urgently. Wires are
generally irreversible once received.
